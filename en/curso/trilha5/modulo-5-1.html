Before the appointment

A preparation is what makes the difference between average and excellent service. With AI, you can review history, prepare questions, and anticipate needs in 5 minutes.

📋 Prompt: pre-appointment preparation

Prepare a briefing for my next appointment: CLIENT: [name] HISTORY: [summarize previous interactions in 2-3 lines] REASON FOR THE APPOINTMENT: [what the client needs today] MY PROFESSION: [your field] GENERATE: 1. Summary of the history (3 bullets) 2. 3 questions I should ask 3. Possible client objections/questions 4. Materials I should have on hand 5. Recommended tone for this client

Do this first

  • ✓ Review client history
  • ✓ Prepare specific questions
  • ✓ Having materials ready

Avoid

  • ✗ Improvise without reading the history
  • ✗ Ask something that has already been answered
  • ✗ Arrive without a defined agenda

During the appointment

During the appointment, AI works as a silent assistant: takes notes, suggests responses, and looks up information. You focus 100% on the client.

🤝 How to use AI during client sessions

  • • Notes: record the audio (with permission) and ask the AI to transcribe and summarize it afterward
  • • Quick consultation: for chat-based customer service, use AI to research information in real time
  • • Suggestions: between client appointments, ask for suggestions on how to approach them

💡 Tip: active listening first

AI is a support tool, not a substitute for active listening. During in-person appointments, maintain eye contact and give your full attention. Use AI between appointments or after the consultation.

Customize your checklist

The generic checklist is the starting point. Customize for your profession, type of service, and client profile.

🔧 Prompt: customize a checklist

Create a personalized client-service checklist: PROFESSION: [your field] TYPE OF SERVICE: [in person / online / hybrid] AVERAGE DURATION: [30 min / 1h / variable] CLIENT PROFILE: [describe in 1 line] GENERATE A CHECKLIST WITH: - 5 items for BEFORE (preparation) - 3 items for DURING (support) - 5 items for AFTER (follow-up) Each item must be a specific ACTION (verb + object). Format: Markdown checkbox (- [ ] action)

💡 Tip: minimum version

Start with the minimum version from the checklist (3 essential items per phase). Add items as the habit takes hold. A checklist that's too long won't get used.

Measuring results

Without measurement, you don’t know whether AI is helping. Simple metrics show the before and after objectively.

📊 4 metrics to track

  • • Time per appointment: how long it takes from preparation to follow-up
  • • Follow-up rate: % of appointments with follow-up sent
  • • Client feedback: % who return or refer others
  • • Hours saved: free time gained per week with AI

⚠️ Don't measure everything

Choice 2 metrics at most to get started. Measuring everything is like measuring nothing. What's important is having a before-and-after point of comparison.
