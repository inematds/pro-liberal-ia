Simple automations: connecting AI to your workflow

The next level after using AI manually is automate repetitive tasks. You don't need to be a programmer: visual tools like Zapier, Make, or even ChatGPT itself with custom instructions can do the job.

1

Custom instructions (GPT/Claude)

Set up standing instructions in your AI tool: your profession, tone of voice, client type, preferred format. Every new conversation starts with context.

2

Automatic email templates

Build a bank of 5-10 sample responses in your email. AI helps create them; you just select one and personalize the client's name.

3

Content scheduling

Use AI to generate a whole month of content in 1 session. Then schedule it using tools like Later, Buffer, or LinkedIn/Instagram’s own scheduler.

4

Zapier/Make for simple workflows

Examples: new lead in the form -> AI generates a personalized welcome email -> sends it automatically. No code.

💡 Tip: start with 1 automation

Don't try to automate everything at once. Choose the most repetitive and tedious task from your week. Automate this one first. Only then move on to the next. Each working automation frees up time and mental energy.

Continuous learning: how to stay up to date

AI changes quickly. New tools, new models, new capabilities. The professional who stops learning in July 2026 is outdated in December. But you don’t need to become a tech influencer to stay up to date.

📚 Recommended sources (15 min/week)

  • • Weekly newsletter: subscribe to 1-2 AI newsletters in Portuguese (e.g., Filipe Deschamps, Manual do Usuario). Read them over breakfast.
  • • LinkedIn: follow 5 professionals in your field who already use AI. See what they post for inspiration.
  • • YouTube: 1 short video per week about AI news for professionals. It doesn't need to be technical.
  • • Communities: join 1 group/forum where independent professionals share AI tips.

Community and sharing: learn from other professionals

Learning on your own works up to a point. Learn from peers speeds things up 3x. Other independent professionals face the same challenges you do and have already found solutions you may never have thought of.

🤝 Where to Find Your Community

  • • Professional association: your OAB, CRM, CRC, or professional council probably has a WhatsApp/Telegram group about technology. If it doesn't, create one.
  • • Events and workshops: attend at least 1 AI event per quarter. It doesn’t have to be a major conference; a local 2-hour workshop counts.
  • • Online groups: communities like Skool, Facebook Groups, or Discord servers specializing in your field + AI.
  • • Mentor or partner: find 1 colleague in your profession who’s also learning AI. Share experiences weekly.

💬 Prompt: find a community

💡 Tip: teach and learn 2x

Advanced Opportunities: AI as a Competitive Advantage

Beyond personal productivity, AI opens up business opportunities for independent professionals. People who master AI can offer new services, serve more clients with quality, and position themselves as experts.

A

Scale customer service

With AI, you serve 30% more clients in the same amount of time. Preparation is faster, follow-ups are automatic, and proposals are ready in minutes.

B

New services

Offer AI workshops for peers, productivity consulting, or premium services with AI integration (e.g., reports with data analysis).

C

Authority positioning

Professionals who publish content about AI in their field become go-to experts. Clients look for those at the forefront.

D

Cost reduction

Less time on administrative tasks = less need for assistants to do repetitive work. Redirect the budget toward what actually grows the business.

Your action plan: the next 30 days with AI

The course ends here. Your journey with AI doesn’t. This last topic is the most important: your concrete action plan for the next 30 days. Without a plan, knowledge turns into forgetfulness in 2 weeks.

[professional challenge 1] 2. [professional challenge 2] 3. [professional challenge 3] GENERATE A 4-WEEK PLAN: - Week 1: focus on [foundation] - Week 2: focus on [practice] - Week 3: focus on [expansion] - Week 4: focus on [consolidation] For each week, list: - 1 main goal - 3 daily tasks (Mon-Fri) - 1 new prompt to try - 1 metric to measure progress

Week 1: Foundation

Set up custom instructions. Save your 5 best prompts. Do your first Monday planning session with AI. Goal: use AI every day, even if only for 10 minutes.

S2

Week 2: Routine

Implement the complete weekly routine (Module 5.2). Produce the first batch of content. Send a follow-up for every service interaction. Goal: complete 5 consecutive days using AI.

S3

Week 3: Expansion

Add 1 simple automation. Try a new use for AI (financial analysis, case study, market research). Goal: do something with AI you’ve never done before.

S4

Week 4: Consolidation

Complete a full review of the month. Measure time saved. Share 1 lesson learned with a colleague. Goal: set goals for month 2.
