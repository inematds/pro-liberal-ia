🏠 ChatGPT — the most popular

O ChatGPT, from OpenAI, is the most widely used AI in the world. It was the first to become popular and has the largest user community. That means more tutorials, more examples, and a better chance that a colleague of yours already uses it. Access it at chat.openai.com.

🏠 ChatGPT's strengths

  • •Simple interface: just type and it responds. No setup required
  • •Custom GPTs: you can create an "assistant" with permanent instructions (paid version)
  • •Plugins and browsing: accesses the internet, generates charts, reads PDFs
  • •Mobile app: available for iOS and Android, with voice recognition
  • •Communities: millions of users, so tips and ready-made prompts are easy to find

Quick test in ChatGPT

You are an experienced [lawyer/doctor/accountant/architect].

Summarize the main care instructions a client should follow in 5 bullet points

should have when [hiring a service / getting a test / filing your taxes].

Use accessible language and avoid jargon.

Paste this prompt into free ChatGPT and see the result in 15 seconds.

💡 Ideal for

Professionals who want get started quickly, who value a mobile app and need a general-purpose tool that does everything reasonably well.

2

🎯 Claude — precision and long context

O Claude, from Anthropic, stands out in three critical areas for independent professionals: long texts, document analysis, and accuracy. It can process documents of up to 200,000 tokens (equivalent to an entire book) at once. Access it at claude.ai.

🎯 Claude's strengths

  • •Huge context window: reads entire long documents without losing information
  • •Projects: you upload reference documents, and it consults them throughout the conversation
  • •Lowest hallucination rate: tends to be more cautious and say "I don't know" when it doesn't know
  • •Detailed analysis: excellent for reviewing contracts, legal opinions, and technical documents
  • •Natural writing: produces less robotic text that feels more human

Real-world use case: Claude Projects

The function Projects lets you upload reference documents (e.g., your professional code of ethics, contract templates, internal regulations). Claude consults these documents in every conversation within the project, without you needing to paste it again.

1. Create a Project called "My Office"

2. Upload your 3 most-used documents (contract template, fee schedule, client FAQ)

3. Ask: "Based on the project documents, draft a response to a client who questions the fee amount."

💡 Ideal for

Lawyers, accountants, and professionals who work with long, complex documents. If you need to analyze contracts, legal opinions, or regulations often, Claude is the natural choice.

3

🔮 Gemini — Google and multimodal

O Gemini, from Google, has a unique advantage: native integration with the Google ecosystem. If you use Gmail, Google Docs, Drive, and Calendar in your office, Gemini works within those tools. Access it at gemini.google.com.

🔮 Gemini's strengths

  • •Google integration: works inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Slides
  • •Multimodal: understands text, images, and video in the same conversation
  • •Real-time research: accesses up-to-date information from the internet
  • •Generous free tier: Gemini 1.5 Flash is free and quite capable
  • •Google Workspace AI: summarizes emails, generates presentations, analyzes spreadsheets

💡 Ideal for

Professionals who already live in Google ecosystem (Gmail + Docs + Drive) and want AI integrated into their existing workflow without having to open another tool.

4

📊 Side-by-side comparison

Here is a direct comparison based on the criteria that matter most to independent professionals. No tool is best at everything. Each has its strengths.

💡 Quick summary

ChatGPT and the Swiss Army knife (does everything reasonably well). Claude is the specialist in long documents and high-quality text. Gemini is the best choice if you already live in Google. Neither is bad. The question is which one fits your workflow better.

5

💰 Free vs. paid

All three tools have a free version. The question is: is it worth paying R$100/month for a premium plan? For an independent professional, almost always. The return pays for itself in the first few hours of use.

Free plan

  • ✓Enough to test and learn
  • ✓Simple, quick tasks
  • ✗Hourly message limits
  • ✗Older/slower model
  • ✗Data may be used for training

Paid plan (~R$100/month)

  • ✓Smarter and faster model
  • ✓More messages per hour
  • ✓Data NOT used for training
  • ✓Advanced features (Projects, GPTs, plugins)
  • ✓Priority access (no queue)

✔️ My recommendation

There isn't just one right answer. But there is a smart way to get started. Here's the path I recommend for independent professionals who are just getting started.

1

Week 1–2: Start with free ChatGPT

It’s the most intuitive and has the most support materials. Use it for simple tasks: emails, summaries, drafts. Get a feel for what it’s like to talk with AI. Don’t worry about writing perfect prompts.

2

Week 3–4: Test Claude with a real document

Take a long document (contract, opinion, regulation) and ask Claude to analyze it. Compare it with ChatGPT. See which result you prefer for your type of work.

3

Month 2: Subscribe to the paid plan of whichever works best

After testing both, subscribe to the one that gave the best results for YOUR tasks. Don’t subscribe to both. One well-used tool is worth more than three underused ones.

⚠️ Common pitfall

Don't compare endlessly. Professionals who spend weeks choosing the perfect tool lose weeks of productivity. The best tool is the one you actually use. Start with any one and refine it later.
