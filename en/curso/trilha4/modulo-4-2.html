💬 Why the right response changes everything

A first response that a client receives shapes their perception of your professionalism. Response time, tone, and clarity build (or destroy) trust before the first meeting. It's the most critical moment in the professional relationship.

📊 Data that matters

  • 78%of clients hire the professional who responds first
  • 5 minis the ideal average response time for business WhatsApp
  • 3x morereferrals when the client feels they received good communication
  • 67%of clients give up if they don't receive a response within 24 hours

✓ What builds trust

  • Quick response (even if partial)
  • Personalized tone (client's name, context)
  • Clear next step (what happens now)
  • Accessible language (without unnecessary jargon)

✗ What destroys trust

  • Slow to respond (more than 24h)
  • Generic message (copy and paste without personalizing)
  • No next step (the client doesn’t know what to do)
  • Cold or overly formal tone

📧 Professional email template

A professional email follows a 5-part structure: subject, greeting, body, next steps, and sign-off. AI generates in seconds what would take 15 minutes to write.

📧 Prompt: first-contact email

Escreva um e-mail profissional de resposta a um potencial cliente:

MEU NOME: [Seu nome]
PROFISSAO: [sua area]
CLIENTE: [Nome do cliente]
ASSUNTO: [O que o cliente perguntou/pediu]
CONTEXTO: [Como o cliente chegou ate voce - indicacao, site, etc.]

ESTRUTURA:
1. Assunto do e-mail (curto, especifico, sem "RE:")
2. Saudacao com nome do cliente
3. Agradecimento + referencia ao contexto
4. Resposta objetiva a duvida/pedido
5. Proximo passo concreto (agendar reuniao, enviar proposta)
6. Despedida profissional + assinatura

Tom: profissional, acolhedor, sem exageros.
Tamanho: maximo 150 palavras no corpo.

💡 Tip: email subject line

The subject line is what determines whether the email will be opened. Use the client name + specific topic. Example: "Maria, tax advisory proposal" works better than "Business proposal." Vague subject lines end up in the trash.

📱 WhatsApp template

WhatsApp is the number one channel for professional communication in Brazil. The difference between a message that converts is one that drives people away because of its structure: short, clear, and with a next step.

📱 Prompt: WhatsApp reply

Escreva uma mensagem de WhatsApp profissional:

CENARIO: [ex: cliente perguntou sobre honorarios]
MEU NOME: [Seu nome]
CLIENTE: [Nome do cliente]
TOM: profissional mas proximo (sem emoji excessivo)

REGRAS:
- Maximo 4 linhas (WhatsApp corta mensagens longas)
- Saudacao com nome do cliente
- Resposta direta a pergunta
- Proximo passo claro (link, horario, ligacao)
- 1 emoji maximo, estrategico

Gere 3 versoes: formal, equilibrada e informal.

✓ Professional WhatsApp

  • ✓Paragraphs of 1-2 lines
  • ✓Client’s name in the greeting
  • ✓Next step with a time
  • ✓1 emoji maximum, well placed

✗ What to avoid on WhatsApp

  • ✗Continuous text without line breaks
  • ✗Too many emojis (looks unprofessional)
  • ✗Long audio recording without prior notice
  • ✗"Dear" or email language

💡 Tip: 3 versions

Asking for 3 versions (formal, balanced, informal) gives you options with no extra effort. Choose the one that best matches the client's profile. Over time, you'll develop an instinct for which tone works with whom.

🔄 Responses for difficult situations

Complaints, delivery delays, fee adjustments. These are the most difficult to write and the ones that have the biggest impact on retention. AI finds the right tone when you can't.

A

Client complaint

Prompt for a customer dissatisfaction scenario

O cliente [nome] reclamou sobre [problema].
Escreva uma resposta que:
(1) Valide o sentimento do cliente
(2) Assuma responsabilidade sem justificativa excessiva
(3) Apresente solucao concreta com prazo
Tom: empatico, firme, profissional.
Canal: [e-mail / WhatsApp]
B

Communicate a delay

Prompt for communicating a missed deadline

Preciso informar o cliente [nome] que a entrega de [servico] vai atrasar [X dias].
Escreva uma mensagem que:
(1) Comunique o atraso sem rodeios
(2) Explique o motivo em 1 frase
(3) Apresente novo prazo firme
(4) Ofereca compensacao se cabivel
Sem vitimismo. Canal: [e-mail / WhatsApp]
C

Fee adjustment

Prompt for communicating a price increase

Preciso comunicar ao cliente [nome] um reajuste de [X%] nos honorarios a partir de [data].
Escreva uma mensagem que:
(1) Valorize a parceria
(2) Justifique com dados (inflacao, aumento de escopo)
(3) Apresente o novo valor
(4) Ofereca opcoes de pagamento
Tom: firme e respeitoso. Canal: [e-mail / WhatsApp]

⚠️ Golden rule

In difficult situations, empathy first, facts next, solutions always. Never start with justifications. Start by acknowledging how the client feels. Then explain. Then resolve the issue.

🤖 Master response prompt

Instead of memorizing dozens of templates, master a single versatile prompt that generates suitable responses for any professional communication situation. This is the prompt that does it all.

🤖 Master prompt: any situation

Voce e meu assistente de comunicacao profissional.
Gere uma resposta para esta situacao:

CANAL: [e-mail / WhatsApp / telefone (roteiro)]
SITUACAO: [descreva o cenario em 2-3 linhas]
CLIENTE: [nome, perfil: leigo / tecnico / empresario]
TOM DESEJADO: [formal / equilibrado / informal]
RESTRICOES: [ex: nao mencionar valores, nao prometer prazos]

REGRAS:
- Se e-mail: assunto + corpo em 150 palavras max
- Se WhatsApp: 4 linhas max, 1 emoji
- Se telefone: roteiro com pontos-chave para a ligacao
- Inclua proximo passo concreto
- Gere 2 versoes para eu escolher

🔑 Why one prompt is enough

  • •O SITUACAO field covers any scenario: from greetings to payment reminders
  • •O TOM field automatically adjusts the language based on context
  • •The CONSTRAINTS keep AI from exceeding its limits
  • •2 versions always: you choose the best one and refine it more quickly
  • •3 channels in a single prompt: email, WhatsApp message, and call script

📁 Response bank

Over time, you accumulate excellent AI-generated responses. Organizing them in a template library lets you reuse, adapt, and share with the team. It’s the difference between individual efficiency and systematic efficiency.

1

Create a "Templates" folder

In Google Drive, Notion, or a folder on your computer. Simple and accessible. The important thing is to have one place for everything.

2

Categorize by type

First contact, follow-up, complaint, price adjustment, thank-you, referral. Use names you can recognize at a glance.

3

Save the best ones

Whenever a response works well (the client responds positively, closes a contract, resolves a conflict), save it with a scenario and channel label.

4

Review monthly

Discard the ones that don’t work, refine the ones that do, and add new ones. In 3 months, you’ll have an arsenal ready for any situation.

💡 Tip: WhatsApp shortcuts

O WhatsApp Business lets you create “quick replies” accessible by shortcut (e.g., /price, /hours, /thanks). Save your best templates there and send them in 2 taps. It’s the fastest way to use your response bank day to day.
