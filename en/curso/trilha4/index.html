📋 Business Proposal Template

Create professional proposals in minutes with templates adaptable to any field.

What it is:

The 7 parts every professional business proposal should have: header, client context, scope of services, methodology, investment, timelines, and signature.

Why learn:

A well-structured proposal conveys credibility and increases conversion rates. Clients compare proposals side by side.

Key concepts:

Professional header, context, detailed scope, transparent pricing, realistic timelines, closing CTA.

What it is:

A structured prompt that takes the client brief and generates a complete, customized proposal in minutes.

Why learn:

Cuts proposal preparation time from hours to minutes, letting you respond to clients faster.

Key concepts:

Prompt with variables, professional context, formal tone, formatted output, fast iteration.

What it is:

A real example of a proposal generated by AI for healthcare professionals: a dental treatment estimate or a medical treatment plan.

Why learn:

Seeing the template in action in your field makes it easier to adapt. Healthcare professionals need language that is both technical and accessible.

Key concepts:

Patient-centered language, cost transparency, treatment steps, informed consent, expiration date.

What it is:

Ready-to-use template for lawyers and accountants to present fees clearly, including the scope of services, payment method, and protective clauses.

Why learn:

Poorly presented fees create distrust. A professional document positions you as an expert and reduces questions.

Key concepts:

Fee schedule, limited scope, payment methods, adjustment clause, response SLA.

What it is:

Techniques for adapting the base template to any independent profession and client type while maintaining a professional structure.

Why learn:

Generic templates don't convert. Personalization shows that you understood the client's specific problem.

Key concepts:

Customization variables, tone by client profile, technical level adjustment, differentiators by field.

What it is:

The complete workflow for reviewing, visually formatting, and sending the proposal to the client, using AI to check for consistency and professionalism.

Why learn:

A proposal with errors or careless formatting destroys the credibility built by its content. Final review is critical.

Key concepts:

Checklist for review, PDF formatting, digital signature, post-send follow-up, and open tracking.

💬 Sample response to a client

Email, WhatsApp, and reply templates for difficult situations. Never get stuck in front of a complicated message again.

What it is:

The direct impact that the tone, speed, and clarity of your response have on the client’s perception of your professionalism.

Why learn:

Clients decide whether they trust you in the first 30 seconds of an interaction. A poor response loses the client before the meeting.

Key concepts:

Response time, professional tone, calibrated empathy, clarity without jargon, implicit call to action.

What it is:

Professional email template with a greeting, context, body, next steps, and sign-off, all adaptable through the prompt.

Why learn:

Well-written professional emails reduce unnecessary back-and-forth and convey organization. AI generates in seconds what would take 15 minutes.

Key concepts:

Effective subject line, empathetic opening, concise body, action-oriented closing, professional signature.

What it is:

Short, direct WhatsApp templates that maintain professionalism without sounding robotic or too cold.

Why learn:

WhatsApp is the main channel for professional communication in Brazil. Poorly written messages cost you clients every day.

Key concepts:

Brief without being cold, short paragraphs, moderate emoji use, clear CTA, response hours.

What it is:

Templates for specific sensitive situations: an unhappy client, a delivery delay, or a fee increase announcement.

Why learn:

These are the hardest messages to write and the ones that have the biggest impact on customer retention. AI helps you find the right tone.

Key concepts:

Empathy first, facts next, a concrete solution, polite firmness, written documentation.

What it is:

A single versatile prompt that generates appropriate responses for any professional communication scenario; just describe the situation.

Why learn:

Instead of memorizing 20 templates, you master one prompt that covers every situation. Maximum efficiency.

Key concepts:

Parameterized prompt, scenario description, desired tone, constraints, fast iteration.

What it is:

A simple system for saving, categorizing, and reusing the best AI-generated responses in your day-to-day work.

Why learn:

Over time, you build a library of tested and approved responses. This makes communication even faster.

Key concepts:

Organization by category, response versioning, usage metrics, team sharing.

🏆 Content template for building authority

Post, article, and short video templates to build professional authority with AI.

What it is:

Professional authority is the perception that you’re a trusted expert in your field. It doesn’t come from a degree; it comes from the content you publish.

Why learn:

Professionals with online authority attract better clients, charge more, and get referred more often. AI makes this accessible.

Key concepts:

Positioning, consistency, area of practice, social proof, educational vs. promotional content.

What it is:

Post structure that educates the audience, drives engagement, and positions you as an expert in your field.

Why learn:

Educational posts generate the most trust and shares. With AI, you can produce one a day in 10 minutes.

Key concepts:

Opening hook, development in bullet points, engagement CTA, strategic hashtags, carousel vs. text.

What it is:

Long-form article and newsletter template to demonstrate technical depth and build a loyal email audience.

Why learn:

Long-form articles build lasting authority. A weekly newsletter keeps you top of mind with clients between engagements.

Key concepts:

Magnetic headline, problem-focused introduction, development with data, conclusion with an opinion, subtle CTA.

What it is:

A structured 30–60-second script for short videos that educate and engage, perfect for professionals who've never recorded a video.

Why learn:

Short video is the format with the greatest organic reach. AI writes the script; you just need to record and post.

Key concepts:

3-second hook, problem-solution in 1 minute, spoken CTA, auto-captions, minimum frequency.

What it is:

A system for planning and generating a whole month of content in a single AI session, including topics, formats, and dates.

Why learn:

The biggest barrier to publishing content is a lack of planning. With the calendar ready, all you have to do is execute.

Key concepts:

Content pillars, format rotation, strategic dates, batch production, repurposing.

What it is:

Guided hands-on exercise to create and publish your first authoritative piece of content using the templates in this track.

Why learn:

Knowledge without action produces no results. Publishing your first piece breaks the inertia and starts the authority-building cycle.

Key concepts:

First post, overcoming perfectionism, analyzing results, iterating based on data.
