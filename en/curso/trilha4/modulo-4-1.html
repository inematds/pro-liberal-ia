📋 Anatomy of the Perfect Proposal

Every professional business proposal follows a structure of 7 essential sections. Whether you’re a lawyer, dentist, architect, or accountant, the structure is the same. What changes is the content in each section.

1

Cover / Header

Your logo, client’s name, date, and service title. First visual impression.

2

Client context

Show that you understood the problem. Restate what the client said in your own words.

3

Scope of services

Detailed list of what's included (and what is NOT). Prevents misunderstandings.

4

Methodology

How you’ll work. Steps, tools, frequency of contact.

5

Investment

Clear prices, payment methods, and terms. No surprises.

6

Deadlines and schedule

When it starts, intermediate milestones, final delivery deadline.

7

Closing and CTA

Proposal validity, instructions for accepting, and contact information.

🎯 Prompt to generate a proposal

O master proposal prompt receives the client's information and generates a complete document following the 7 sections. Copy it, fill in the variables in brackets, and paste it into your AI.

📋 Prompt: Generate a business proposal

You are a commercial proposal writer for independent professionals. Generate a complete professional proposal with these 7 sections: PROFESSIONAL DETAILS: - Name: [Your full name] - Profession: [e.g., Tax attorney] - Contact: [phone and email] CLIENT DETAILS: - Name: [Client/company name] - Problem: [Describe in 2-3 lines what the client needs] - Context: [How the client found you] GENERATE THE PROPOSAL WITH: 1. Professional header with logo (describe where to place it) 2. Context — paraphrase the client's problem 3. Scope — list the included AND excluded services 4. Methodology — numbered work stages 5. Investment — amount, payment method, terms 6. Timeline — schedule with milestones 7. Closing — valid for 15 days, instructions for accepting TONE: professional, confident, without unnecessary jargon. FORMAT: ready to paste into Word/Google Docs.

Copy this prompt, replace the fields in brackets, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

✓ What to DO in the prompt

  • ✓Provide real client context (not generic)
  • ✓Specify the desired tone
  • ✓Ask for a ready-to-use format

✗ What NOT to do

  • ✗Ask "write a proposal" without providing data
  • ✗Forgetting to mention the excluded scope
  • ✗Send without reviewing amounts and deadlines

👨‍⚕️ Example: treatment proposal

Healthcare professionals (doctors, dentists, physical therapists) present treatment estimates that work as business proposals. AI generates the complete document from a simple brief.

🏥 Template: dental estimate

Generate a professional dental treatment estimate: PATIENT: Maria Silva, 45 years old COMPLAINT: pain in tooth 36, visible fracture DIAGNOSIS: crown fracture with pulp involvement TREATMENT PLAN: - Root canal treatment on tooth 36 - Cast metal core - Porcelain-fused-to-metal crown Include: professional introduction, diagnosis explained in accessible language, treatment steps with duration, detailed cost for each procedure, payment options (pay in full with a 10% discount, 3 or 6 interest-free installments), valid for 30 days, pre-treatment instructions. Tone: warm and professional. Patient is not a dental professional.

💡 Tip: accessible language

Always ask AI to use language the patient can understand. Terms like "endodontics" become "root canal treatment." Patients need to understand what will happen to confidently accept the estimate.

👩‍⚖️ Example: fee proposal

Lawyers, accountants, and consultants present fee proposals that define the scope, fees, and terms of the service. A well-written proposal prevents future conflicts and conveys professionalism.

⚖️ Template: attorney's fees

Generate a legal services fee proposal: ATTORNEY: Dr. Carlos Mendes, OAB/SP 123.456 SPECIALTY: Business Law CLIENT: Empresa ABC Ltda, CNPJ 12.345.678/0001-90 REQUEST: Review of 5 service agreements, drafting of a new corporate agreement Include: - Law firm introduction (2 lines) - Description of included AND excluded scope - Fee table by service - Payment terms: 50% upon engagement + 50% upon delivery - SLA: response time of 24 business hours - Confidentiality: basic clause - Validity: 10 days - Digital signature Tone: formal, objective. Client is a business owner.

✓ Include in the proposal

  • ✓Exact scope of services
  • ✓Prices by item/stage
  • ✓Response time (SLA)
  • ✓Annual adjustment clause

✗ Explicitly exclude

  • ✗Services outside the contracted scope
  • ✗Court costs and third-party fees
  • ✗Service outside the agreed hours
  • ✗Reviews beyond the specified number

✏️ Customizing the template

A generic template doesn’t convert. The personalization is what turns an ordinary proposal into one that makes the client feel it was made just for them.

🎨 Customization variables

Add these instructions to the prompt to adjust the template:

  • •Technical level: "The client is a layperson / has technical knowledge / is another professional in the field"
  • •Tone: "formal and distant / formal and warm / informal and friendly"
  • •Urgency: "urgent request, highlight immediate availability"
  • •Differentiator: "mention X years of experience in the field / Y certification"

💡 Tip: include the client’s name in the text

Include the client name at least 3 times throughout the proposal (context, scope, and closing). Sales research shows that using the person’s name increases acceptance rates by up to 22%.

📤 From Draft to Sending

The proposal generated by AI is a draft. Before sending, go through a review checklist. AI can also help with this step by checking consistency, tone, and possible errors.

✅ Final review checklist

Review this business proposal as a senior consultant: 1. DETAILS: Are the client's name, amounts, and dates correct? 2. SCOPE: Are what's included and excluded clearly stated? 3. TONE: Is it professional without sounding distant? 4. JARGON: Are there technical terms without explanations? 5. CTA: Does the client know exactly what to do to accept? 6. FORMATTING: Are the paragraphs short, headings clear, and the proposal easy to read? 7. VALIDITY: Is the proposal's expiration date specified? Point out problems and suggest specific corrections.

Never send without reading. AI can invent data, get values wrong, or use the wrong tone. You're the professional; AI is the tool. You are always responsible for the final content.
