☀️ Start of the day

The first 10 minutes of the day with the assistant, set the pace for everything. You open the chat, paste your schedule and the previous day's outstanding tasks, and ask for a morning briefing. The AI cross-references information, organizes it by priority, and tells you exactly where to start. The result: instead of spending 30 minutes reading emails and trying to remember what’s still pending, you start the day already knowing what to do.

📋 Morning Briefing Prompt (Copy and Use)

Good morning. Here is my schedule for today and yesterday’s outstanding tasks:

SCHEDULE:
[paste today’s appointments]

YESTERDAY’S OUTSTANDING TASKS:
[paste what was left incomplete]

Please:
1. Organize by priority (urgent / important / can wait)
2. Suggest the best order of execution
3. Identify scheduling conflicts
4. List the materials I need to prepare before each appointment
5. Estimate how long each task will take

Format: numbered list, one line per item, with a suggested time.

📧 Communication

Communication takes up 30-40% of the day of a solo professional. Emails, WhatsApp messages, client replies, follow-ups. With the assistant configured to match your voice, that time is cut in half. The workflow is simple: you describe what you need to say, the AI generates text in your style, you review it in 30 seconds, and send it.

📧 Formal Email

For clients, colleagues, and government agencies.

Tone: formal. Structure: greeting > context > body > next step > sign-off.

💬 Professional WhatsApp

Quick responses, confirmations, reminders.

Tone: approachable. Short and direct, with moderate emojis if that’s your style.

📣 Announcements

Team announcements, circulars, newsletters.

Tone: institutional. Clear and objective, with a defined CTA.

📋 Quick Email Prompt (Copy and Use)

Write a professional email with this information:
- To: [name and relationship - e.g., "Dr. Carlos, client since 2023"]
- Subject: [main topic]
- Goal: [what I want to communicate or ask]
- Tone: [formal / approachable / urgent]
- Details: [additional information that should be in the email]

Use my usual communication style. Maximum 150 words.

💡 Tip: response in 3 steps

For any message that needs a reply: (1) paste the original message into the chat, (2) say "reply to this for me, [formal/approachable] tone", (3) review and send. The whole process takes less than 1 minute per message.

📝 Production

Technical documents are where independent professionals spend the most creative energy: reports, opinions, assessments, legal filings, proposals. With the assistant, the workflow changes radically. You stop staring at a blank page. The process becomes: raw notes in, structured draft out, human review at the end.

1

Input: raw data

Your meeting notes, case data, numbers, observations. It can be loose text, bullet points, or even transcribed audio. AI accepts any input format.

2

Processing: structured prompt

You ask: "Turn these notes into a [legal opinion/assessment/report] in the format I use, with the standard sections." The assistant already knows the format because it's in the context.

3

Output: structured draft

A document ready with 80–90% of the final content. The heading, body, analysis, conclusion, and recommendation are already in place. You review the remaining 10–20%: technical details, data validation, signature.

📋 Prompt to Turn Notes into a Document (Copy and Use)

Transforme estas anotacoes brutas em um [TIPO: parecer / laudo / relatorio / proposta]:

ANOTACOES:
[cole suas anotacoes aqui - pode ser texto solto, bullet points, audio transcrito]

DADOS DO CASO:
- Cliente/Paciente: [nome]
- Data: [data]
- Contexto: [breve descricao]

Use o formato padrao que definimos. Inclua todas as secoes obrigatorias. Mantenha meu tom profissional. Se alguma informacao estiver faltando, indique com [COMPLETAR].

🤝 Customer service

Arriving prepared for a client meeting changes the client’s perception. AI does in 2 minutes the summary you would spend 15 minutes reading medical records, case files, and previous notes to prepare. The result: you go into the appointment, meeting, or hearing already knowing the history, outstanding items, and key questions you need to ask.

✓ With AI preparation

  • ✓Case summary in 30 seconds
  • ✓History of previous interactions
  • ✓Suggested key questions
  • ✓Possible next steps already listed

✗ Without preparation

  • ✗15 minutes rereading old notes
  • ✗Risk of forgetting important details
  • ✗Questions thought up on the spot, without a script
  • ✗The client may get the impression of improvisation

📋 Pre-Appointment Briefing Prompt (Copy and Use)

Preciso me preparar para um atendimento. Aqui estao os dados:

CLIENTE/PACIENTE: [nome]
TIPO: [consulta / reuniao / audiencia / retorno]
HISTORICO: [cole anotacoes anteriores, resumo do caso]
OBJETIVO DESTE ENCONTRO: [o que preciso resolver/discutir]

Me de:
1. Resumo do caso em 3 linhas
2. Pendencias anteriores que preciso abordar
3. 5 perguntas-chave para este encontro
4. Possiveis proximos passos apos o atendimento
5. Alertas (prazos proximos, riscos, pontos sensiveis)

📊 End of the day

Ending the day with clarity keeps pending tasks from being forgotten. AI turns your scattered notes into an actionable summary for the next day. In 5 minutes, you know what you did, what’s still pending, which follow-ups you need to send, and what to prepare for tomorrow. The result: your next day starts organized.

📋 Daily Wrap-Up Prompt (Copy and Use)

Aqui esta o que fiz hoje e as anotacoes do dia:

TAREFAS CONCLUIDAS:
[liste o que voce fez]

TAREFAS PENDENTES:
[liste o que ficou para depois]

ANOTACOES SOLTAS:
[cole qualquer anotacao do dia - reunioes, ideias, lembretes]

Me de:
1. Resumo do dia em 3 linhas
2. Lista de pendencias para amanha (por prioridade)
3. Follow-ups que preciso enviar (para quem, sobre o que)
4. Preparacao necessaria para os compromissos de amanha
5. Um lembrete se algum prazo importante esta proximo

💡 Tip: context transfer

Save the day's summary as the first message of the next day. That way, the assistant "remembers" what happened yesterday and can pick up where you left off without you having to explain everything again. This creates a chain of context that grows richer over time.

🔄 Gradual integration

Trying to change everything at once leads to frustration. G gradual adoption lets you validate each step before moving on, ensuring AI actually helps instead of getting in the way. The plan below works for any independent profession. Follow one week per phase.

S1

Week 1: Communication

Low risk, high impact

Start by using AI to draft emails and messages. It’s the safest task (you review before sending) and the one that saves the most time. Goal: 5 emails a day assisted by AI.

S2

Week 2: Document production

Moderate impact, learning curve

Add the creation of draft documents: opinions, reports, proposals. Start with simple documents and gradually increase the complexity. Goal: 2 documents drafted by AI per week.

S3

Week 3: Preparing for client meetings

High impact on client perception

Use the pre-appointment briefing before each appointment or meeting. Bring the case history and ask for a summary + suggested questions. Goal: prepare 100% of appointments with AI.

S4

Week 4: Complete routine

Full integration into the workflow

Add the morning briefing and daily wrap-up. Now you have the complete cycle: start the day with AI, communicate with AI, produce with AI, prepare with AI, and end the day with AI. Assess: how much time are you saving per week?

⚠️ Signs that it’s working

  • You are spending less than 5 minutes per email (average)
  • Document drafts need less than 20% rewriting
  • You don't forget pending tasks from one day to the next
  • Clients say you “remember everything” during appointments
  • You are freeing up 1-2 hours a day for strategic work
